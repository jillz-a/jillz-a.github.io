Privacy policy

rclone homelab is a personal backup tool used by a single person, the owner of the Google account it connects to.

What it accesses

It requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. That means it can see and change only the files and folders it created itself (encrypted backup files). It cannot read any other files in Google Drive.

What it stores or shares

No data is collected, stored or shared by this app or its author outside the owner's own Google Drive and home server. Nothing is sold, analysed or passed to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
